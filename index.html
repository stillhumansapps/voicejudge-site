How it works

Three steps to stardom. Or chaos.

One round takes about a minute. No script to learn, no singing, no account.

  1. Pick a scene

    Choose from 40 original scenes in 6 packs, from talking pets to anime showdowns. Or import a short clip of your own.

  2. Dub it

    Watch the scene once. Then the original voice is muted and it's your turn: follow the line and the timeline cues and give it everything.

  3. Get judged

    The judge scores your timing, words and delivery, drops a verdict and a tip for next time. Beat your friends, or beat yourself.

A fair (and funny) judge

Scores for timing, words and delivery, plus a verdict line and a tip on what to try next. Every take gets a laugh.

Dub your own clips

Import a short video from your phone (5 to 60 seconds), mute it, and turn your own moment into a scene.

Tonight's scene

A different scene to dub every day, right on the home screen. Quick warm-up, instant bragging rights.

Replay, save and share

Replay your last take, then save your dub as a video and send it to friends. Saving comes with Judge Pro, or unlock a save with a short ad.

Private by design

No account. Your voice is judged on the phone, and your takes and imported clips stay on your device.

Judge Pro (optional): ad-free rounds, save dubbed videos to Photos and import clips up to 3 minutes. The core game is free.

Challenge your friends

Your score vs. theirs.

Share your dub as a video. It shows your scene, the judge's verdict and your score, plus a short link to the same scene.

  1. Dub a scene and share it to your story, chat or group (saving the video needs Judge Pro or a short ad).
  2. Your friend opens the link (like voicejudge.app/c/K3) and lands on that exact scene.
  3. They dub it too. The judge decides who takes the crown.
